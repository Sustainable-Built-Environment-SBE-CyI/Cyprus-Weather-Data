# Data licence and attribution

## Source data

The meteorological observations archived in `data/` originate from:

- Cyprus Department of Meteorology
- National Open Data Portal of Cyprus
- Dataset: Current Meteorological Station Observations (API)
- Source endpoint: `https://dom.org.cy/AWS/OpenData/CyDoM.xml`

The source dataset is published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International licence (CC BY 4.0).

Required attribution:

> Contains current meteorological station observations supplied by the
> Cyprus Department of Meteorology through the National Open Data Portal of
> Cyprus, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

The source observations are preliminary and may later change following
quality control. Station outages may cause missing or erroneous values.
